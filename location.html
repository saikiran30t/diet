How to Find Us

Landmarks & Directions

From Airport

~45 minutes drive from Rajiv Gandhi International Airport. Take the Outer Ring Road towards Nizampet.

By Metro

Nearest metro station is Miyapur (Blue Line). From there, it's a 10-minute auto ride to our center.

By Bus

TSRTC buses ply frequently to Nizampet. Get down at Nizampet Bus Stop and walk 5 minutes to Hyma Arcade.

Parking

Ample parking space available at Hyma Arcade. Valet parking assistance provided.
